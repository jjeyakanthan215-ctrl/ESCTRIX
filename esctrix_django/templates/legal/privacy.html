Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is Our Priority

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Last updated: August 2026 · Effective immediately

Age Requirement — 18+ Only. ESCTRIX is intended solely for users who are 18 years of age or older. By creating an account, you confirm that you meet this age requirement. Accounts found to belong to minors will be immediately terminated and data deleted.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide you with a secure, private chat experience.

  • Account data: Username, display name, date of birth (used for account verification only — never shared).
  • Messages: Messages are end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) on your device. We cannot read your messages — ever.
  • Usage metadata: Online/offline status timestamps, last-seen time (can be hidden in settings).
  • Support tickets: If you contact support, we store your submitted message to assist you.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your real name, phone number, or email address (unless you choose to provide one).
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties or advertisers.
  • We do NOT use tracking cookies or advertising pixels.
  • We do NOT store your messages in readable form — all messages are encrypted.
  • We do NOT have access to your cryptographic keys — they are generated on your device.

3. How We Protect Your Data

ESCTRIX applies multiple layers of protection to keep your account secure:

  • All messages use End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) via the WebCrypto API — no server can read them.
  • Passwords are stored using Django's PBKDF2 + SHA-256 hashing algorithm — never in plain text.
  • All connections use HTTPS/WSS (TLS) to prevent man-in-the-middle interception.
  • Sessions expire automatically after extended inactivity.
  • Database is hosted locally with strict access control.

4. Your Rights

  • Right to access: You can view all information stored about your account in your profile settings.
  • Right to delete: You may request full account deletion by contacting ESCTRIX support. All messages and data will be permanently erased.
  • Right to correction: You can edit your username, display name, and bio at any time from settings.
  • Data portability: You may request an export of your account data via the support team.

5. Disappearing Messages

ESCTRIX offers a Vanish Mode where messages automatically self-destruct after a set timer (10s, 30s, 1 min, 1 hour, or 1 day). Once a message disappears, it is permanently deleted from the server and cannot be recovered by anyone, including ESCTRIX administrators.

6. Children's Privacy (18+ Policy)

ESCTRIX is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will immediately terminate the account and delete all associated data.

7. Contact & Data Requests

For any privacy-related queries, data deletion requests, or concerns, please submit a support ticket after logging in, or use the Help form on the login page. We respond to all privacy requests within 72 hours.
